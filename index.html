Timefields Ads Reporting

Timefields Ads Reporting is an internal reporting tool operated by Timefields Media Group Inc., a digital marketing agency. It lets our staff read Google Ads reporting data for advertising accounts that our clients have authorized us to access, so we can monitor delivery and prepare performance analysis for those clients.

The tool only reads data. It does not create, change or pause ads, budgets, billing or account settings, and connecting it grants no access beyond what the signed-in user already has in Google Ads.
